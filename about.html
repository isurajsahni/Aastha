About Aastha Diagnostic Centre

Pioneering diagnostic excellence, precision, and compassionate healthcare across Ludhiana and Punjab for over 15 years.

OUR JOURNEY

Committed to Accurate & Reliable Health Reports

Founded in 2011, Aastha Diagnostic Centre has grown into one of Punjab’s most trusted diagnostic destinations. We combine medical expertise with state-of-the-art automated analyzers to deliver precise results when health decisions matter most.

Whether it is routine preventive blood screening or complex pathology profiling, our facility adheres to international NABL protocols, barcoded sample management, and strict internal quality assurance.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and 100% accurate diagnostic solutions with compassionate patient care.

Our Vision

To lead healthcare diagnostics through innovation, automation, and uncompromising quality standards.

Our Core Pillars of Excellence

The principles that guide our everyday diagnostics and patient care.

Precision & Accuracy

Fully automated robotic pathology systems ensuring error-free sample processing and reporting.

Patient First Approach

NABL Quality Standards

Rigorously validated protocols and external quality assessment controls for all blood parameters.

Fast Turnaround Times
