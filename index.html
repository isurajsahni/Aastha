Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our diagnostic services.

How do I book a home sample collection?
You can book a home sample collection by calling our helpline, using the 'Book Home Collection' button on our website, or sending us a message on WhatsApp.
How long does it take to get test reports?
Most routine blood and urine test reports are available within 12-24 hours. Specialized tests may take 2-3 days. We will notify you via SMS when reports are ready.
Can I access my reports online?
Yes, you can easily download your reports from our website by entering your patient ID/Mobile Number and password provided at the time of booking.
Do I need to fast before my blood test?
Some tests like Fasting Blood Sugar and Lipid Profile require 10-12 hours of fasting. Our team will guide you on any specific preparation needed when you book the test.
Are the test results accurate?
Absolutely. We use highly advanced automated equipment and run regular quality control checks to ensure 100% accuracy in our diagnostic reports.
